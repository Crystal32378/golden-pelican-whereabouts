# 作品集海報（每個作品風格不同）

> 檔名沿用先前的 slop.md，但「slop」是誤稱——這些是有品味的小作品。

與 golden-pelican 系列的「案件卷宗 / 目擊現場」風格刻意區隔：
這裡每個作品用**它自己的調性**，不做統一視覺系統。

原則沿用已驗證的做法：
- 資訊文字用中文或英文都可以，但**逐字列出**並要求不得出現其他文字
- 主體是**照片**或**平面物件**，不是白紙上排版
- 輸出 3:4（1152×1536）

## BV-01｜線上打小人出氣筒 · 祭壇主場景

```
A photograph of a small paper altar at night, shot on a phone with slight
motion blur, as if the person holding it had just swung something. The altar is
a folding table covered in dark red and gold paper, creased and worn. Two
candles burn at the left and right edges, their flames streaked by the motion
blur. In the centre stands a flat cut-out paper figure of a stick-thin office
worker, already torn at one shoulder, a smear of red ink across the chest. A
rubber slipper lies in front of the altar, mid-air, dropped. Rising smoke
curls up through the frame. The room behind is dark and cluttered with
newspaper.

Printed on a paper banner taped across the top of the altar in brush
calligraphy, the characters large and confident:

拖鞋一拍，晦氣消散

Below the altar, in small stamped type:

12 個預設小人 · 4 種法器 · 20 句咒語

No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## BV-02｜線上打小人出氣筒 · 法器與傷害值

```
A photograph of four objects laid out on a dark red cloth, lit from one side by
a single candle so that half of them fall into deep shadow. From left to
right: a cheap blue plastic slipper, an extra-long steel sewing needle, a
nine-section metal whip, and a wooden sword carved with faded characters. Each
one is worn, chipped or scratched. A small red paper card stands behind each
object, and a thin trail of smoke passes over all of them.

Each red card is printed with a single large number, and the numbers increase
left to right:

-1    -2    -3    -5

Below them, in a single stamped line:

怒氣集滿 100，桃木劍連環五擊

The leftmost slipper is the cleanest and the rightmost sword is the most
worn, as if the sword is the one that has been used most. No other text
anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## UP-01｜神經痛就醫導航 · 症狀地圖

```
A photograph of a wall-mounted clinic wayfinding sign, matte white vinyl on a
concrete wall, shot straight on under even fluorescent light. The sign is
large and clinical, screwed to the wall at four corners, one corner slightly
lifted. A scuff mark runs across the lower edge. Everything is calm, cold and
evenly lit, the opposite of a poster.

The sign carries a clean grid of small pictograms, each in its own box, in a
single flat blue-grey. The pictograms are simple line drawings: a hand with
sparks, a foot with pins, a face wincing, a bolt of electricity, a flame. One
box in the grid is marked with a small red dot.

Across the top, in plain sans-serif capitals:

SEE A DOCTOR WHEN

Below the grid, in a single line:

麻 · 刺 · 燒 · 觸電感

And at the bottom, smaller:

神經內科 · 復健科

The sign is legible, calm, and slightly worn, like something in a hospital
corridor. No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## UP-02｜神經痛就醫導航 · 寫在牆上的字

```
A photograph of a corridor wall at night, lit only by a low emergency light
near the floor, so the wall is mostly dark and the light rakes across it at a
low angle. The wall is painted a pale green that has yellowed unevenly. A
sheet of A4 paper is taped to it, and someone has written on the paper in
large, slow, careful handwriting with a biro — the writing presses hard enough
to gouge the paper. The sheet is the only bright thing in the frame. A bench
edge is visible at the bottom, out of focus.

The handwritten text reads:

我以為會自己好
已經三個月了

不改變, 不知道, 要不要去

寫在下面，字小很多：

那就去看

The three lines are not evenly spaced, as if the writer paused between them.
The Chinese characters are the only non-Latin text in the image. No other text
anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## TT-01｜真話翻譯機 · 原文與人話對照

```
A photograph of a single sheet of white printer paper on a desk, shot from
directly above under even light. The paper has been printed on one side and
folded once, so the top half lies slightly over the bottom. The upper half
contains dense, formal Chinese text in a small serif face, evenly set, the way
an official notice is printed. The lower half has been written on by hand in
red ballpoint, much larger and much less formal, going over the fold.

The printed text in the upper half is legible and reads:

為優化整體服務品質，我們將於近期進行
相關調整，敬請體諒並持續給予支持。

The handwritten translation in the lower half reads:

我們要改了。
你會不方便一點。
但我們不會說是哪裡不方便。

A red ballpoint pen lies diagonally across the fold. The contrast between the
two hands is the whole point of the image. No other text anywhere. No
watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## TT-02｜真話翻譯機 · 五種譯法

A photograph of five narrow strips of paper laid out side by side on a grey

## PS-01｜THE PROMPT SANG ITSELF · 五個聆聽站

```
A photograph of a listening station in a dark room: a shelf of five objects,
each one a different piece of discarded studio equipment, lit only by a small
clipped lamp above the middle of the shelf. From left to right: a battered
cassette recorder with its tape door hanging open, a coiled audio cable on a
hook, a pair of studio headphones with the padding crushed flat, a spiral
notebook with a page torn out, and an empty reel lying on its side. Dust in the
lamp beam. The wall behind is unpainted concrete. Everything is monochrome
except the reel, which is a faded red.

A strip of masking tape is stuck along the front edge of the shelf, and on it
someone has written by hand, in pencil, five numbers and five short labels,
spaced to line up with the five objects. The writing is small and drifts
downward slightly, as if written without looking up. The labels read:

1 PROMPT
2 TRACK
3 MISREAD
4 RECORD
5 LATER

The pencil lines under three of the five labels have been gone over twice, as
if the writer changed their mind and pressed harder. No other text anywhere. No
watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## PS-02｜THE PROMPT SANG ITSELF · 指令變成樂譜

```
A photograph of a single page of music manuscript lying on a dark wooden desk,
shot from directly above under a warm lamp. The page is cream and slightly foxed
at the edges. On it, printed stave notation in black ink, a short melody in
four bars. But the stave has been overprinted: across the printed notes, in
blurred black as if printed by a machine reading a text field, there is a line
of English text set on the staff itself, sitting where lyrics would normally
go. The two overlap and neither one fully wins.

The overlaid text on the staff reads exactly this:

sorry, I think this part is yours now

The staves are unmarked otherwise. A pencil lies at the bottom edge of the
frame, and a crumb of eraser beside it, as if someone tried to take the words
out and gave up halfway. Warm, quiet, and slightly wrong. No other text
anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## WC-01｜Walk Me There · 貓頭鷹與路口

```
A photograph taken at a real street corner at dusk, from the pedestrian's own
point of view, as if standing there about to be uncertain. A narrow pavement,
a kerb, a crossing with worn paint, a street sign seen from below at an angle
so its lettering is hard to read. The light is that flat blue-grey just after
sunset. A takeaway cup and a crushed can sit on the kerb.

Perched on top of the street sign, small in the frame and lit from the side, is
a small owl. It is not a cartoon: it has real feathers and real talons, and it
is looking directly down the lens, at the person holding the camera, rather than
along the road. Its head is tilted very slightly, the way an owl does when it
is listening rather than deciding. The owl is the only warm thing in the
picture.

The only text in the image is on the street sign, and it is read from below at
an angle, so the letters are foreshortened and only partly legible:

ONE WAY

No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## WC-02｜Walk Me There · 走錯的三十秒

```
A photograph of a phone screen lying face-up on a wet pavement, the screen
cracked in one corner, photographed from above. Rain has beaded on the glass
and one large droplet has run across the top of the display, blurring whatever
was under it. The map shown on the screen is simple and high-contrast, with a
single dotted route line and one solid position dot; the route line runs off
the bottom edge of the screen, so where it actually goes is not visible. A
revolving blue navigation arrow sits on the dot, slightly off true north, as if
it cannot quite settle.

Beside the phone on the pavement, a single set of wet shoe prints, and next to
them, a second set going the other way. The two sets overlap and then separate
again. The pavement is dark and reflective, and the only light comes from off
frame, throwing the prints into long shadow.

The only text on the phone screen is a single line at the top, cut off by the
droplet so that only the end of it is clear:

...recalculating

No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## DO-01｜Draw One · 抽籤

```
A photograph of a wooden table surface in low warm light, shot from directly
above. On the table, a single folded paper object, made of thick handmade paper
folded into a small flat envelope shape, and left slightly open at one corner.
Next to it, unrolled, a long strip of the same paper with printed columns
running down it, the columns too small to read but clearly ruled. Beside that,
a plain terracotta bowl, and a single dark ink brush resting across the rim. A
teacup ring marks the wood. A small pool of tea has been left and is drying at
the edge.

The folded paper object is the only thing in sharp focus. Light falls on it
from the upper left and the fold shadows fall to the right. The whole
arrangement is sparse and deliberate, with a lot of empty table around it, the
way a tea table is set for one person.

There is no text anywhere in the image except faint ruled column marks on the
paper strip, which are not legible. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## DO-02｜Draw One · 問與答

```
A photograph of a sheet of rice paper laid on a dark lacquered desk, shot from
directly above under a single warm lamp. The paper is a soft off-white with
visible fibre flecks. In the centre of the sheet there is exactly one ink
brush stroke: a single deliberate horizontal line, slightly thicker at the
left where the brush pressed down and tapering as it was drawn to the right,
with a small amount of ink pooling at the end. The rest of the sheet is empty.

A stone-paperweight shaped like a small fish rests on the top left corner of
the sheet. A cup of ink with a stained rim sits to the right, and beside it a
brush that is still wet. The edge of the lamp's light falls across the sheet
and stops, leaving the lower third in shadow.

Above the single stroke, small and very neat, one short question is written in
faint pencil:

今天要往哪裡走

Below the stroke, in the same pencil, very small:

不要問第二次

There is no other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## RC-01｜Reel Crew · latent film library

```
A photograph of a film archive room, shot down the middle of a long aisle. Metal
shelving runs away from the camera on both sides, stacked to the top with
labelled archive boxes of uniform size, some slightly pulled out. The aisle is
lit by a single strip of fluorescent light running along the ceiling, so the
light falls in a hard line down the middle of the floor and the shelves at the
edges go into darkness. The floor is bare concrete with a painted yellow
guideline running along it, worn and crossed by trolley marks. A rolling
library ladder stands half-way down the aisle, empty.

Every box is labelled with a printed index label of exactly the same size and
same layout, in the same position, on every single box, going as far as the
light reaches. The labels are all identical and none of them can be read at
this distance. A few boxes have been re-labelled, and the newer labels sit
slightly crooked over the old ones.

The point is the sheer number of identical boxes. No other text is legible
anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## RC-02｜Reel Crew · 我們已經有什麼

```
A photograph of a wall of index cards, shot straight on. The wall is entirely
covered, floor to ceiling and edge to edge, with hundreds of small cards in a
card-catalogue cabinet, each card in its own slot. Most of the cards are
labelled with printed type of exactly the same length. A few slots are empty,
and several cards have been pulled part-way out and left sticking out at
different angles, as if someone has been working through them.

In the centre of the wall, in a gap where cards have been deliberately removed
to make room, someone has pinned a single larger card by hand. It is the only
card on the wall that looks different, and it is the only one in colour. It
carries one handwritten line, and the handwriting is large and slow and
presses into the card:

我們已經有什麼

A pencil is tied to the cabinet with string, hanging at the height of that
gap. Everything else on the wall is out of focus. No other text is legible. No
watermark, no logo, no website.
```
