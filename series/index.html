Several posters carry Chinese characters the model added on its own. They are sometimes in the wrong script and sometimes nonsense, and they have been left exactly as they came out. The Office files what it receives; it does not correct it. One citation is also dated three years before the crime.

Where a poster names a place, you can go and stand there. Most of them do not — so those ones ask instead, and point at the map. If you can place one, the Office would like that.
