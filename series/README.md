# A bird rode a bicycle through 24 places in 24 hours

Sixteen posters from the Golden Pelican case file, in two alternating styles.

- **Style A — the Office.** Case files, citations, evidence bags, a typewriter.
- **Style B — the world outside.** Witness statements, pencil sketches, notices
  left where somebody found something.

Every poster contains the bird or the bicycle, sometimes only as a sketch, a
reflection, or a wheel track. The Chinese characters in several posters are the
model's own additions. They are sometimes in the wrong script and sometimes nonsense,
and they have been left exactly as they came out — the Office files what it receives
and does not correct it.

All images generated with **Hy Image 3.5 preview** (Tencent Hunyuan) and checked
by OCR afterwards.

## Things people made because of the bird

Four more images at the bottom of the page — merchandise. Nobody authorised them.
Four people saw the bird, or a photograph of it, and each went to a different workshop
and asked for something. They did not know about each other and they do not agree: the
woven label has six spokes and a crooked bird, the 3D print has a beak that is too long,
the sketchbook has a photograph of the real places pasted on by hand, and the postcards
were printed from whatever the printer thought those places looked like.

Only five things are actually locked — the pelican, the wheel, the red, the case file
feeling, and being absurd in a completely official tone. Everything else is allowed to
drift, because a version where all four match is the version where somebody is in
charge, and somebody being in charge is the opposite of this page.

Sold by preorder: you pay first, each maker has a minimum order before the workshop will
start, and if the minimum is not reached in 60 days the money goes back.

Prompts for these four are in `../docs/posters/merch.md`.
