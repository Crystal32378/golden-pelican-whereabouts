# 黃金鵜鶘 品牌識別系統（Brand System）· 第一批 6 張

沿用作品既有的**兩個聲音**，把它制度化（不是從零發明）：

- **Office 側（機關）**：卷宗、印章、公文紙、識別證、證物袋標籤 → 工整、可複製、紙
- **外界側**：布章、車衣、水壺、貼紙、傳單 → 手作、街頭、布料與墨水

## 通則

- 比例 **3:4**（1152×1536）
- **主角規則（硬性）**：每張必有**鵜鶘或紅色單車** —— 剪影、倒影、插畫、徽章裡的圖形都算
- **標誌內不得出現任何文字**（含中文）——【Crystal 2026-09-29 決定】「鵜鶘」是低頻字，模型會寫錯，
  而標誌會被複製一百次，錯誤會跟著被複製一百次。**mark 只准是圖形**；文字一律走 wordmark 的拉丁字母
- **資訊文字全英文**；中文只出現在「錯了也無害」的位置（浮水印、牆上的字、印章），不進 mark、不進 wordmark
- 色票：紙白 `#F4F1EA` · 近黑 `#2B2D33` · 印章紅 `#B8322A`
- 字體對：**襯線（聲音）＋ 等寬（資訊）** —— 沿用 series 頁現有的組合
- 每張結尾固定：除列出的文字外不得出現任何其他文字；不要水印、不要網址

## 質感原則的例外（重要，與 Reddit 系列相反）

Reddit 系列的教訓是「粗糙的手寫感要從照片紙張與光線自然帶出，不要叫模型寫潦草一點」。
但**品牌本體**（標誌、印章、標籤、識別證）**應該是工整的** —— 識別系統的價值就是可複製。
所以這裡刻意分邊：**Office 側要求印刷體的精確**，只有**外界側**（傳單、粉筆字）才用手寫質感。
這是有理由的例外，不是忘記教訓。

## BR-01｜標誌總表（mark sheet · Office 側）

```
A brand identity sheet lying flat on a pale paper surface under soft daylight,
photographed straight down, like a page from a working brand manual. Six cells in
a neat grid, each showing the same mark in a different form.

The mark is a roundel: a red bicycle wheel seen from the side, with a white
pelican silhouette standing on it above, wings level, enclosed by a thin circle.

CRITICAL: the roundel is a picture and nothing else. Inside the circle there is
only the wheel, the bird and the line of the circle. There is no banner, no ribbon,
no plaque and no writing of any kind inside the roundel — no Latin letters and no
characters of any alphabet. A mark with anything written in it is the wrong mark.

1. the roundel in full colour
2. the same roundel in one colour, solid black ink
3. the roundel reversed out in white on a solid red square
4. the roundel small, about the size of a fingernail, next to a coin for scale
5. a lockup: THE GOLDEN PELICAN set in tall condensed capitals, with a line of
   small monospaced type underneath reading THE VILLAGE JEALOUSY OFFICE
6. the mark used badly, crossed out with a red pencil: stretched, tilted,
   rotated, filled with a gradient

The six captions are the only words on the sheet. Set them in small monospaced
type, perfectly legible, one under each cell, on their own lines:

PRIMARY MARK
ONE COLOUR
REVERSED
MINIMUM SIZE 8 MM
LOCKUP
DO NOT

The sheet has minor handling marks: a paperclip shadow and one soft crease.

No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```

## BR-03｜公文紙（letterhead · Office 側）

```
A photograph of a single sheet of official letterhead paper lying on a dark
wooden desk, shot from slightly above in warm afternoon light, with a pen and a
pair of reading glasses just outside the frame of the paper.

The letterhead is printed in black and one shade of red. Across the top, in tall
condensed capitals:

THE VILLAGE JEALOUSY OFFICE

Below it, in small monospaced type, set on two separate lines with a real line
break between them so the three figures are not run together into one sentence:

DEPARTMENT OF UNRESOLVED SIGHTINGS
24 REPORTS / 0 ARRESTS / 1 BIRD

A thin red rule runs the full width of the page under the heading. At the lower
right, a small roundel printed in red: a bicycle wheel seen from the side, with a
pelican standing on top of it, wings level. It is a bird perched on a machine —
there is no person, no rider and no human figure anywhere in the roundel.

Under the rule, a short block of typescript, perfectly legible:

Our file on this matter remains open. We are unable to confirm
whether the subject is one bird or several. Witnesses have been
unhelpful and, in two cases, apologetic.

The paper is slightly yellowed at one edge, as if it has been in a drawer.

No other text anywhere. No watermark, no logo, no website.
```
